# knowledge.log.md

Decisions, not touches. Only the last entry is soft — refinements fold into it; the next
entry freezes it. Frozen entries are never rewritten; corrections and reversals are new
entries referencing the old. Consult by entry, not end-to-end. (Full rule:
`knowledge.vision.md`.)
