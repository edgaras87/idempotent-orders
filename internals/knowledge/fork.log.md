# fork.log.md

The fork's record: changes to `core/` and `realization/`, and why — never this node's
own decisions, never the problem. Log law per `knowledge.vision.md`: decisions, not
touches; only the last entry is soft; frozen entries are never rewritten — corrections
and reversals are new entries referencing the old. Flow-back to the workbench is this
log plus the branches' diffs against upstream.

**0001 — Realization skeleton reshaped: prepare-thinking-part, and need-driven map
adoption.** First deviation of the fork.
`realization/workbench-backend-realization.map.md` amended in place, two revisions:

1. **The problem's reasoning chain grouped.** Former steps 2, 3, and 6 (project
   intent, project defined, first slice chosen) re-carved into one composite
   initialization step, **`prepare-thinking-part`**, with inner steps project-intent,
   system-definition, and slice-registry born — the registry populated from the
   definition's concern map with the first slice marked chosen-next, folding the
   former first-slice step into the registry's birth. Why: the three are one nature
   of work — the problem's reasoning, the thinker's whole territory under the
   narrow-roles hypothesis — while the surrounding steps (node bootstrap, working
   ground) are different natures; the map's steps now mirror role territory. The
   living registry stays execution's control point; only its birth is
   initialization's. Marked unlived; open questions recorded in the step (does the
   first-slice fold-in survive lived work; does the role-mirroring grouping principle
   generalize).

2. **Scheduled instantiation replaced by need-driven adoption.** The former step 4
   ("realization mapped" — the skeleton instantiated as a separate project map)
   removed from the step sequence; in its place a *Map adoption* section: the project
   works against its fork copy directly, molding it under the fork rules; execution
   state lives entirely in the project's record, coupled to the map by shared names
   only (record entries self-contained — reference-to-read never required); a
   separate live map artifact is born only when project-specific, non-knowledge
   redrawing would pollute the fork's diff against upstream. Why: this skeleton is
   itself untested — a scheduled copy would fork an unlived picture into a second
   artifact ahead of any need, against earned-by-need; molding the fork copy is what
   the fork mechanism exists for, and keeps one map until lived work demands two.
   The map-discipline preamble softened to match (names-coupling instead of the
   status pointing readers into the map). Marked unlived.

Owed confirmation, correction, or reversal at this project's finalization pass;
flow-back to the workbench batched per the vision's fork rules.
